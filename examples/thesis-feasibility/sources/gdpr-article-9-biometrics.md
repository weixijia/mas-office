> "Processing of biometric data for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person shall be prohibited unless explicit consent or legal authorization applies."

Relevance: R3 — Identifying individual guests via biometric gait profiles falls under special category personal data requiring explicit prior guest consent under GDPR and UK GDPR.
